Sticky Notes Plus

A quiet sticky-notes app for Windows, with reminders that can reach your phone through Google Calendar.

What it does
  • Colored sticky notes on your desktop, with a notes board, search, archive and restore.
  • Rich text, checklists, images, and dates with an optional time and repeat.
  • Windows notifications when a reminder is due.
  • Optional one-way sync of timed reminders to a Google Calendar the app creates, so you are alerted on your phone when you are away from your PC.

Google Calendar sync

Sync is off until you choose Connect Google Calendar in Settings. The app then asks Google only for permission to manage calendars it creates itself, and adds a calendar named "Sticky Notes". It cannot see or change your other calendars or events. Disconnect at any time from Settings.

Your notes stay on your PC. See the privacy policy for exactly what is sent to Google.
