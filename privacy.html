Privacy Policy

Sticky Notes Plus · Effective October 29, 2026

Summary

Sticky Notes Plus keeps your notes on your own computer. The developer runs no servers and collects nothing. The only data that leaves your computer is reminder information you choose to sync to your own Google Calendar.

Data stored on your computer

  • Notes, images, reminders and settings are stored in a local database in your Windows user profile.
  • If you connect Google Calendar, the access token Google issues is stored on your computer, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) so only your Windows account can read it.
  • There is no analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting.

Google Calendar sync

Sync is off unless you choose Connect Google Calendar. When connected, the app requests a single permission:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created — create and manage calendars made by this app.

With it, the app creates a calendar named "Sticky Notes" and manages events only in that calendar. It cannot read or change your other calendars or events.

For each reminder that has a time, the app sends to Google:

  • the first line of the note, as the event title;
  • the note's text, as the event description — unless you turn off Include note text, in which case only the title is sent;
  • the reminder's date, time, time zone, repeat rule and alert time.

Images, attachments, date-only reminders and all other notes are never sent. Sync is one-way: the app does not read event contents back from Google.

Data is sent directly from your computer to Google over HTTPS. It is not sent to the developer or any third party, is not sold, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Sticky Notes Plus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Stop syncing: Settings → Google Calendar → Disconnect. This revokes the app's access and deletes the stored token. Tick Also delete the Sticky Notes calendar to remove its events from Google.
  • Revoke from Google: at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete local data: uninstalling the app removes the program; your notes database can be deleted from your Windows profile.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
